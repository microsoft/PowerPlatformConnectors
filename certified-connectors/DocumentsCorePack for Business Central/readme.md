## DocumentsCorePack for BC

DocumentsCorePack BC enables you to create and process documents using Microsoft Word layouts and Business Central data. 
Integrate document processes into your workflows with a no-code approach and work with documents and layouts directly from your automated processes.

The connector provides a no-code way to work with Business Central documents and layouts directly from Power Automate.

[Details](https://short.mscrm-addons.com/documentscorepackbc)

Follow [this link](https://short.mscrm-addons.com/dcpconnectorbc) to see step-by-step tutorials, how-tos and get detailed information.

## Supported Operations

### Create document
Creates a new document based on Business Central data and a selected layout.

### Create DocumentJob (async) 
Creates a new request to generate a new document based on Business Central data and a selected layout. The action does NOT wait for a result.

### Get DocumentJob result
Retrieves status information of a DocumentJob. The result will include the generated document.

### Get DocumentJob status
Retrieves the status of a DocumentJob.

### Get document templates
Retrieves a list of available DocumentsCorePack layouts.

### Get Profiles for a User APIKey
Retrieves all Profile Ids for this APIKey.

### Get Printers
Retrieve available printers.

### Who am I
Retrieve information about the used APIKey and associated DocumentsCorePack Service.
